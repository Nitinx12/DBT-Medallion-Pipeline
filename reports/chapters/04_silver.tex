\chapter{Silver}
\chapterlead{Nine models turn raw bronze into typed, deduplicated, FK-resolved tables.}

\begin{glance}
\item All silver models are incremental: \file{merge} on natural keys, \file{append} for lookups.
\item A 3-day lookback plus \file{ROW_NUMBER} dedupe absorbs late-arriving updates.
\item Lookup tables (\file{brands}, \file{categories}, \file{payment_methods}) resolve text to surrogate keys.
\end{glance}

\section{Cleaning patterns}

Entity models dedupe with \file{ROW_NUMBER() PARTITION BY} natural key ordered by \file{updated_timestamp DESC, created_timestamp DESC, _id DESC}, cast text to \file{INT}/\file{NUMERIC}/\file{TIMESTAMP}, normalize phones to \file{+1-XXX-XXX-XXXX} (splitting \file{phone_extension} on \file{x}), map \file{Y} to boolean, and concat names. Lookup models distil distinct normalized values with stable surrogates:

\begin{wmlisting}{dbt/models/silver/brands.sql (excerpt)}{wm jinja}
{{ config(
    materialized='incremental',
    incremental_strategy='append'
) }}

WITH source_brands AS (

    SELECT DISTINCT INITCAP(TRIM(brand::VARCHAR)) AS brand_name
    FROM {{ source('bronze', 'products') }}
    WHERE
        brand IS NOT NULL
        AND TRIM(brand::VARCHAR) <> ''
\end{wmlisting}

\file{products} and \file{orders} LEFT JOIN their lookups on the normalized text, turning denormalized strings into nullable FK ids. Tests in \file{dbt/models/silver/schema.yml} pin the contracts: unique plus not-null PKs, nullable-aware relationships, non-negative ranges, an email regex (warning) and the \file{line_amount = round(quantity * unit_price, 2)} expression (error).

\begin{keybox}
\item Silver is the single place where source quirks get fixed; gold and the dashboard assume clean input.
\item Incremental merge plus lookback keeps reruns cheap and late data correct.
\item Nine standalone SQL checks in \file{tests/data-quality/silver/} re-verify the same ground outside dbt.
\end{keybox}
