\section{Design decisions}

\file{fact_order_items} is the only incremental gold model, with the same 3-day lookback as silver for late lines. Dimensions full-refresh because rebuilding from silver is cheap at this scale and avoids SCD bookkeeping where history is not needed; policy is Type-1 everywhere (overwrite). Every table stamps \file{gold_loaded_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP} for lineage. The star snowflakes only at products into brands and categories, which the brand/category reports read directly.

\file{employees} stops at silver: headcount analytics is out of scope, so no \file{dim_employees} exists (also recorded in \file{gx/suites/gold_suites.py}). Order-header measures such as \file{total_amount} live on \file{dim_orders}; there is no \file{fact_orders}. Gold is gated by \file{dbt test --select gold} plus the seven standalone checks in \file{tests/data-quality/gold/}.

\begin{keybox}
\item Type-1 full-refresh dims plus one incremental fact is the cheapest correct shape at this scale.
\item Grain statements above are the contract; row-count parity tests enforce them.
\item No employees dimension is a decision, not an omission.
\end{keybox}
