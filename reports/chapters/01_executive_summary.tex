\section{Problem and solution}

Analytics needs typed history and dimensions; MongoDB stays write-optimized. The pipeline bridges them: PySpark extraction with watermark upserts lands raw bronze, nine dbt models clean it into silver, eight models star it into gold, and a Streamlit app serves seven pages from one shared join graph. Three independent quality systems (dbt tests, SQL suite, Great Expectations) gate each hand-off, and per-table contracts record the agreed shape against the live database.
