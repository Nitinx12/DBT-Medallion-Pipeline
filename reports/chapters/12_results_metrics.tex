\chapter{Results and metrics}
\chapterlead{Every chart below is generated from \file{reports/data/*.csv}; nothing is hand-drawn from memory.}

\begin{glance}
\item Component, test and row-count CSVs come from \cmd{uv run python scripts/report/collect_metrics.py}.
\item Durations come from the newest \file{logs/pipeline_*.log}; unknown cells stay blank.
\item Bronze counts of one row per table are the seeded snapshot recorded in the contracts, not production volumes.
\end{glance}
